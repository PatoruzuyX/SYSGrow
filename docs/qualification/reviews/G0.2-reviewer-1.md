8.2 G0.2-AG-01 — Physical qualification authority contradiction
High — Blocking
Observed conflict

AGENTS.md, L17 states:
No real device operation or live private data in qualification.

However, Guidelines, L38 states:
Hardware-in-loop tests require separate owner approval, controlled equipment and qualified device-specific limits.

Further, QUALIFICATION-PROFILES.md, L10 explicitly requires separately authorised HIL evidence within device-specific safety qualification.

The blanket prohibition in AGENTS.md conflicts with the conditional physical qualification permitted elsewhere.

Counterexample

A future reviewer receives explicit authorisation to conduct controlled hardware-in-the-loop qualification of a device-specific safety mechanism. The qualification profile requires real equipment evidence, and the engineering guidelines permit controlled HIL testing under separate approval.

AGENTS.md nevertheless prohibits real device operation during qualification without limiting that prohibition to read-only or synthetic profiles.

The reviewer must stop because the repository's instruction-precedence policy prohibits selecting the least restrictive conflicting instruction.

Architectural consequence

This is not a runtime vulnerability or evidence of an unsafe physical operation. It is a governance deadlock capable of blocking required safety evidence even after the owner authorises an appropriate controlled qualification session.

Required correction

Replace the blanket prohibition with profile-specific restrictions. Recommended wording:
Read-only, documentation and synthetic software qualification must not operate real devices or access live private data. Device-specific hardware-in-the-loop qualification requires separate explicit owner authorisation, competent assessment, controlled equipment, established safety limits and a bounded qualification plan. Such authorisation does not grant production activation authority.

Reconcile this wording with SECURITY.md and the qualification profiles. Preserve the complete prohibition on physical operations during G0.2.
