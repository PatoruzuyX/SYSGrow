## Mandatory future evidence-preservation requirements (proposed normative rules)

1. Before work: record task ID, dated explicit authorization, exact repository/PR/base/HEAD/tree identity, applicable instructions/ADR/plan, scope/exclusions, local/remote preflight, evidence profile and accountable author/reviewer. Capture stable source URLs and content hashes or immutable Git identities; distinguish raw evidence and derived analysis.
2. During work: preserve original reports, reviewer submissions, tool/command and environment records, test fixtures/seeds, exact checkout and expected/actual outcomes, complete redacted logs and CI run/job/context IDs, skips and failures. Capture source path/line ranges and exact revision, tool result provenance and limitations. Record measured/simulated/planned/not-run distinctly. Avoid private data, credentials, live telemetry, device identifiers and unsafe material in public records. Store sensitive originals in owner-approved access-controlled durable storage; record sanitized references and checksums without exposing secrets.
3. At checkpoint: archive original bytes where safe, manifest file identity, SHA-256 (or Git object identity with algorithm labeled), author/date, provenance, extraction method and verification result; stable repository or controlled evidence store references, not chat-only or ephemeral CI artifact links. Record artifact retention and retrieval/restore test, accountable custodian, scope and known missing evidence. Do not fabricate hashes or raw results; unavailable mandatory evidence is HOLD, not N/A.
4. Before integration: independently verify raw/derived integrity, completeness, finding mapping and exact candidate identity. Keep final exact-HEAD CI/review/owner approval records outside candidate tree in the canonical PR record; preserve stable links and durable copies/checksums with explicit supersession. Record actual CI checkout tree correlation. A changed HEAD requires applicable fresh evidence/review. No self-approval, automatic merge or waiver.
5. After integration: record resulting revision, ancestry/content equivalence, approvals, residual restrictions and evidence location; retain original records and adverse results. Corrections add dated superseding records rather than overwriting originals. No deletion of historical refs or evidence without separate owner-approved retention/security disposition and preserved traceability.
6. Evidence retention: owner defines durable storage/retention and access controls before work starts; preserve qualification artifacts at least for the life of the dependent release/decision and until replacement/supersession is independently verified and owner-approved. Test retrieval before relying on archived evidence. If preservation fails, stop dependent qualification and record recovery work; never retroactively infer missing results.
